On this International Women’s Day, I’m taking the time to reflect on the women in my life who have shaped and empowered me — I say without a doubt I am the product of incredibly strong women.

My mom, started her dream business when most wish to retire. She has never conformed to traditional gender roles and she’s damn proud of that.

This year, my partner, pushed her body through so many limits just to bring new life into this world — and she makes it feel like it’s no big deal. She’ll go back to helping assess the competency of violent offenders in couple months

My mother-in-law didn’t hesitate to give an organ of hers to her husband last year and was back at work in less than a month.

My grandmother, who herself wasn’t allowed an education, vowed her four daughters who get one — All four have Ph.Ds.

I have countless women/non-gender conforming co-workers who continue to inspire me. When I decided to look for companies to [join this year](https://medium.com/@sunils34/pivoting-to-civic-tech-and-nava-9b0eadf0225a), one of the criteria I judged companies on was how many women and underrepresented groups do I get to work with. Now, nearly every leader I work with including my boss are women, and each of them push me in ways it’s truly hard to keep up.

It leads me to conclude this world would truly be a better place with more women in charge.

I know won’t ever achieve parity in understanding of what it’s like to be a woman today, but I can say I won’t ever stop fighting to help make society a little bit better for you.
